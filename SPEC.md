# GOTHAM COLLAGE: SPEC (v2)
Gotham Collage (GC) is a new student-led venture studio. Website concept: a dark, cinematic, futuristic secret headquarters. Flow: THE PORTAL, then THE GOTHAM ELEVATOR (replaces the menu), then THE STORY, then THE FINAL SCREEN. If any earlier prompt conflicts with this file, this file wins.

LOOK
- Base: black (#0b0b0b), charcoal, white and metallic silver. ONE accent: warm gold (#F5B800) with a soft amber glow (rgba(245,184,0,0.5)). No other colors, no gradients other than glows, no cards, no crowded layouts.
- Gold is used sparingly (under 10% of any screen) and only for: thin vertical light strips, one arc of the portal rings, button outlines and their glow, small uppercase eyebrow labels, the word "HERE." in the hero headline, the selected floor button, the "Learn More" link, and the light ring around Project 001 and the final cube. Everything else stays white/silver/black.
- Lighting is the star: glowing rings, thin light paths, reflective black floors, soft fog. Match the mood and layout of the mockup in /reference.
- Typography: wide geometric uppercase display font for headings (Unbounded or Syncopate from Google Fonts), clean sans (Inter) for body. Text off-white (#e8e8e6), never pure white on big areas. Small labels are uppercase with letter spacing.
- Logo: the real file in /reference (silver/white G with a smaller G inside). Use as is, never redraw it.

COPY (use exactly)
- Brand spelling: "Gotham Collage" (never "College").
- Top nav: Projects / The Elevator / About / Join Now, plus a gold-outlined button "Enter Portal" at the top right (it jumps to the portal fly-through).
- Hero label: WELCOME TO GOTHAM COLLAGE
- Hero headline: IDEAS ARE EVERYWHERE. (white, big) / FOUNDERS ARE BUILT [HERE.] ("HERE." in gold)
- Sub line: A venture studio built by ambitious minds.
- Hero button: Enter Our World → (gold outline). Scroll hint: "Scroll Down".
- Elevator lobby: eyebrow THE ELEVATOR (gold). Title: CHOOSE YOUR FLOOR. Sub: Explore the depths of Gotham. Floor panel shows 04 / 03 / 02 / 01 from top to bottom, up/down arrows beside the doors, the selected floor filled gold.
- Floor 01 ABOUT GOTHAM: Gotham Collage is a student-led venture studio where ambitious students come together to build real-world projects. We believe in learning by building and creating things that solve real problems.
- Floor 02 THE METHOD: BUILD (we collaborate as students and build projects from scratch) / IDENTIFY (after launching and validating projects, we identify people with the potential to become founders) / CREATE (we work with selected founders to turn successful projects into independent startups through equity partnerships). Line: We don't just teach entrepreneurship. We build real things.
- Floor 03 PROJECT ARCHIVE: eyebrow PROJECT ARCHIVE (gold). Title: PROJECT 001. Pill: STATUS : IN DEVELOPMENT. Text: Our first project is in the works. Stay tuned. Link: Learn More → (gold). Scene: a dark faceted obsidian orb on a pedestal inside a glass display case with a thin glowing orbit ring and small blurred "classified file" panels. "Learn More" opens a small panel that says only: "Status: in development. Details will be shared when we launch." Do not invent any project details.
- Floor 04 OUR VISION: eyebrow OUR VISION (gold). Title: FROM STUDENTS TO BUILDERS. FROM BUILDERS TO FOUNDERS. Small line under it: We want to create an ecosystem where students don't have to wait for opportunities to build something meaningful. Button: Join The Movement → (gold outline). Scene: the G inside a glass cube on a pedestal with a gold ring of light and a city skyline.
- Story (minimal, shown after floor 04): We are a group of students who want to build real projects rather than simply discuss ideas. We start by creating and launching projects together. When a project shows potential, we identify the right people to take it forward as founders. We are starting small, but our ambition is much bigger. We don't claim to be an investment fund or a venture capital firm. We are builders creating our own opportunities.
- Final line: THE FUTURE IS BUILT, NOT WAITED FOR.
- Footer: logo + GOTHAM COLLAGE on the left; centre text: Building Projects. Creating Founders. Launching Startups. Right: social icons.

SOCIAL ICONS
Show an icon only if its URL is set in config. Only LinkedIn has a URL now (https://www.linkedin.com/company/gotham-collage). X, YouTube and Discord stay hidden until I add real URLs. Never link to a fake or placeholder page.

HARD RULES
- Never call GC a VC firm or investment fund. Never promise funding, returns or outcomes.
- No equity percentages anywhere.
- No invented team members, testimonials, user counts, partners, project details or other companies' logos.
- "Join Now" and "Join The Movement" link to the LinkedIn URL in config. Colors, fonts and all URLs live in one config file; do not change them without asking me.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Must work on phones with a lighter effects mode. All text is real HTML.
